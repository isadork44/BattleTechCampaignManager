Company roster

+ Add a unit by hand
Use this form for salvage or units bought off the books; the Marketplace adds purchases here automatically. 'Mechs, vehicles, fighters, VTOLs, support vehicles and battle armor each need a tech team: hire teams on the Personnel page, then pick one in the Tech team column. A unit without a team can't start repairs. Each unit also pays spares (50 × tonnage; fighters 75 × tonnage for fuel and ordnance). Support vehicles take a MechWarrior; infantry, battle armor, VTOLs and emplacements pay crew wages instead of a pilot salary; infantry and emplacements need no tech team. Hulks pay no spares until restored. The team's level is its repair-roll modifier (Green −1 · Regular +0 · Veteran +1 · Elite +2).

Pilots

+ Hire or add a pilot
Hiring bonus = 2 months' salary — log it in Finances.
MechWarriors crew 'Mechs, combat vehicles and support vehicles (Savior, MASH Truck, APCs, artillery carriers); aerospace pilots fly fighters. Both use the same ratings, salaries, XP and wounds. XP awards: battle 1 · kill 1 (max 2) · objective 1 · standout 1 · contract complete 1. One skill improvement per pilot per month; wounds heal day by day in the Infirmary (7 days each with an assigned carer, 14 without). 6 wounds = KIA.

Hiring hall candidates this month

Quality 2d6: 2–6 Green · 7–9 Regular · 10–11 Veteran · 12 Elite. Signing bonus = 2 months' salary. Hire a candidate with "+ Hire or add a pilot" above, setting their role and skills.

Tech teams

+ Hire a tech team
One team keeps one unit running and works one repair job at a time. Teams are paid every month whether or not they're assigned: Green 1,500 · Regular 2,000 · Veteran 3,000 · Elite 4,500. The level is the repair-roll modifier (−1 / +0 / +1 / +2). Veteran and Elite teams are only on the market when the 2d6 ≥ 9 roll succeeds.

Support staff

Tech teams have their own list above. Basic admin, food and medical are covered by the 10% overhead — list only extra hires here. Two roles are wired straight into the rules: a Negotiator automatically adds +1 to every contract negotiation, and each Surgeon or Medic can care for one wounded pilot in the Infirmary (1 wound per 7 days instead of 14) — hire one for every pilot you expect to be hurt at once.

Medical staff

Healing and repairs both tick as days pass. End Month runs the clock to day 30.

Patients

Wounded pilots can still deploy. A pilot heals 1 wound every 7 days while a carer is assigned to them, every 14 days without. Each surgeon or medic (hired on Personnel) treats one patient at a time; an operational MASH Truck treats up to four. Carers are released automatically when their patient is fully healed. Six wounds is fatal. Wounds from battle are recorded in the Debrief; use + and − here for anything else.

Inner Sphere — 3049

Positions are approximate. JumpShips cover 30 LY per jump and recharge for about a week between jumps — travel costs 2,500 C-bills per 'Mech per jump. System type sets the Marketplace modifier: Periphery −2 · Standard +0 · Major industrial +2.

Reputation control

Reputation adjusts itself: resolving a contract applies its result (+1 Success · +2 Exceptional · −1 Failure · −2 Breach), and losing more than half the roster's 'Mechs during a contract adds −1 automatically at resolution. Use these only for DM overrides — story events, employer commendations, war-crimes tribunals…

⚙️ Cloud config (only if you want a different database)
  1. Go to console.firebase.google.com and create a free project (no credit card needed).
  2. In the project, open Firestore in the left sidebar → Create database (any region).
  3. In Rules, paste the rule shown in this package's README, then Publish.
  4. From the project overview, click the </> web icon (or Project settings → General → Your apps → Add app → Web) and copy the firebaseConfig object it shows.
  5. Paste that whole object below and click Save config — then send the same block of text to the rest of your group so everyone points at the same database.

Monthly backups

Every press of End Month automatically captures the company as it was before payday and stores it in this browser (last 12 kept, refresh-proof). Load one to rewind to that month, or Download a copy to keep off-site.

⚠ Reset campaign

This wipes everything — roster, pilots, contract, ledger, reputation — back to the default sample company. It cannot be undone. Consider copying a save from Save / Load first.
