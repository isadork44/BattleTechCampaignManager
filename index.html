BattleMech roster

Each 'Mech carries one tech team (Green 1,500 · Regular 2,000 · Veteran 3,000 · Elite 4,500 /mo) and spares of 50 × tonnage. Hulks pay no spares until restored. Tech team quality is also the repair-roll modifier (−1/+0/+1/+2).

MechWarriors

Hiring bonus = 2 months' salary — log it in Finances.
XP awards: battle 1 · kill 1 (max 2) · objective 1 · standout 1 · contract complete 1. One skill improvement per pilot per month; wounds heal 4/month at payday (medical care assumed). 6 wounds = KIA.

Support staff

Tech teams are managed on the 'Mech roster (one per 'Mech). Basic admin, food and medical are covered by the 10% overhead — list only extra hires here. Two roles are wired straight into the rules: a Negotiator automatically adds +1 to every contract negotiation, and a Surgeon or Medic automatically provides medical care in the Repair Bay (1 wound per 7 days instead of 14).

Reputation control

Reputation adjusts itself: resolving a contract applies its result (+1 Success · +2 Exceptional · −1 Failure · −2 Breach), and losing more than half the roster's 'Mechs during a contract adds −1 automatically at resolution. Use these only for DM overrides — story events, employer commendations, war-crimes tribunals…
